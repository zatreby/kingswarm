# What players say about the closest comparables (community voices) — for Kingswarm

Researched 2026-09-30. Method: 10,048 English Steam reviews pulled by API from 16 games (200 most-helpful positive + 200 most-helpful negative all-time, plus the 200 most recent of each polarity; 330 to 765 per game), then 7,194 coded by keyword themes (only reviews dated 2024-09-30 or later, except Tooth and Tail, Just King, The Unliving and The Wild at Heart, where too few are recent, so all dates). Recall is imperfect and negatives are over-sampled on purpose (each polarity is reported separately). Counts tagged **[C]** are regex counts; **[H]** are hand-classified from the regex hits; quotes are exact Steam text (link = the review). What players SAY is quoted; what I conclude is tagged **[I]**. Reddit was not reachable (HTTP 403 on JSON, login wall on RSS, pullpush 429): **Reddit = UNVERIFIED, no data**. Hacker News (3 comments) is used only as a cross-check. Raw data: `.cache/community/rev/*.json` (gitignored).

## Bottom line for Kingswarm

1. **Keep the run at 12-15 min and never grow it; 15-20 min is where players say the ceiling is.** Halls of Torment's 30-minute runs are the loudest length complaint in the sample (31 of 506 recent reviews say so [H]; reviewers say the store page sold "quick and casual 30 minute runs"). Players name a target: "Runs need to be 15-20 minutes max. They are 30 minutes" ([halls 212871854](https://steamcommunity.com/profiles/76561197960949048/recommended/2218750/)). AGREES with GDD §7 and `retention.md` #3; mildly DISAGREES with `genre-teardown.md` #7 (15-25 min run). Also NEW: much of the complaint is dead time inside a run ("Takes like 10minutes to clear the map of items, then you just stand there for literally 20minutes" ([halls 209726842](https://steamcommunity.com/profiles/76561198023322867/recommended/2218750/))), so every battle needs an escalation beat, which Frenzy and Crown-open already are.
2. **The draft is the right hook, but only if every pick is a real choice.** "One more run" language appears in 17% of survivors-like positives [C], and the reasons are build variety ("trying more weird weapons and crazy combos" ([vs 212397080](https://steamcommunity.com/profiles/76561198206754344/recommended/1794680/))) and improving after death. The threat is "false choices": 9 Vampire Crawlers reviews, plus Brotato and Halls ("no real decision to be made" ([halls 186212189](https://steamcommunity.com/profiles/76561198124643361/recommended/2218750/))). AGREES with `retention.md` #1-2; NEW: bot-sim every Mutation for pick rate and win-rate lift, and flag any that is picked more than ~60% or under ~5% of the time when offered.
3. **Small games are punished for repeating the opponent or place, more than for a small pick count; Kingswarm's six authored rivals are the right axis.** About 100 coded reviews (65 negative) say enemies, bosses or maps repeat: "Only 3 maps - Not enough enemy variety" ([20mtd 209780133](https://steamcommunity.com/profiles/76561198123004160/recommended/1966900/)); "there are more builds that you can make than enemy variety, the ratio is like 30:1" ([brotato 220216912](https://steamcommunity.com/profiles/76561198065266432/recommended/1942280/)). 20 Minutes Till Dawn has 12-13 characters, 11 weapons and 24 runes and still drew 20 content complaints in 453 reviews; Brotato and Vampire Survivors drew about 0.5%. Count is not the fix either: Rounds has 69 cards, yet a reviewer says "you saw already all possible perks" ([rounds 216073917](https://steamcommunity.com/profiles/76561198025487559/recommended/1557740/)) after ~30 min. AGREES with GDD §6; NEW: treat 22 Mutations (31 pieces with heroes and abilities) as the floor, not the goal, make each rival visibly different in play, and make rival variants and a second layout per arena the cheapest Crown Level extension.
4. **At $3-8 a 5-hour game is forgiven; at $15 it is the top complaint; a promised-then-abandoned roadmap is worse than both.** Just King, after 2.5 hours (2022, early access): "Totally worth the $4 I paid" ([justking 119787518](https://steamcommunity.com/profiles/76561197994030688/recommended/1059980/)). Bad North at $15: "isn't good enough for a thumbs up at this price point" ([badnorth 190402418](https://steamcommunity.com/profiles/76561197995291974/recommended/688420/)). The single biggest negative theme in any small game was abandonment: 88 of 217 recent 20 Minutes Till Dawn negatives (41%) [C] ("the fully paid PC version is dead" ([20mtd 213798446](https://steamcommunity.com/profiles/76561198357428939/recommended/1966900/))). NEW: launch at or under $5 (or free on web), ship a complete 1.0, and promise no roadmap. Consistent with `distribution.md` (VS at $2.99 start).
5. **Versus must ship with a non-cheating vs-AI; do not depend on a matchmaking pool.** Tooth and Tail: 55 of 310 negatives (18%) attack the AI/campaign, and 31 of 666 reviews mention AI cheating or unfair asymmetry ("the AI does not obey any of the limitations placed on you" ([tnt 234617362](https://steamcommunity.com/profiles/76561198025746001/recommended/286000/)), 2026). Rounds: no CPU opponent ("They didn't even add a CPU" ([rounds 234966030](https://steamcommunity.com/profiles/76561198316001207/recommended/1557740/))) and "getting matched up with an opponent can take several minutes in vanilla" ([rounds 228213962](https://steamcommunity.com/profiles/76561199160723825/recommended/1557740/)). AGREES strongly with `fun-ai.md` #3 and #5 and GDD §6 (same input struct, no cheats) and `distribution.md` (couch first); NEW: online = invite link only, no queue.
6. **Versus draft: offer best-of-3 or 5 by host choice, allow a rematch that keeps the draft, and test combo snowball.** Rounds players complain about the length cap (on the 5-round cap, "just when things get chaotic, it starts all over" ([rounds 213972300](https://steamcommunity.com/profiles/76561199652402465/recommended/1557740/))) and 19% of recent reviews mention mods, 25 of those 97 for more players, longer rounds or lobby options ("mods are almost required for this game for basic qol changes like a continue button, more then 2 players at a time" ([rounds 184176000](https://steamcommunity.com/profiles/76561199227617885/recommended/1557740/))). Balance is 12% of Rounds negatives: "once your opponent has grow +explosions, its over" ([rounds 188171294](https://steamcommunity.com/profiles/76561198924284712/recommended/1557740/)). DISAGREES mildly with fixed "best of 3" in GDD §8; NEW otherwise. Start Versus with Duo and Brutes off until bots show no combo above ~60% win rate.
7. **Crown Levels must be opt-in, additive and tactic-changing, never stat inflation.** Brotato's Danger 0-5 is loved; its May 2026 "Nightmare" update was not: negatives went from 15 of 473 (3%) in April to 47 of 387 (12%) on 13-31 May [steam window API], with "The new update is awful. It turns the game from a balanced challenge into a one hit and you die bullet hell" ([brotato 225368952](https://steamcommunity.com/profiles/76561197976493972/recommended/1942280/)). Veteran voice: "The game was fun because you could experiment" ([brotato 225854149](https://steamcommunity.com/profiles/76561198363953658/recommended/1942280/)), then builds collapsed to pure damage. AGREES with `retention.md` #5; NEW: bot-test that at Crown 8 at least 3 Mutation builds still win.
8. **The boss must reward the build, not invalidate it.** 34 of 220 recent Thronefall negatives (15%) mention the boss or ending [C]: "Every strategy you've come up with becomes irrelevant" ([thronefall 177287815](https://steamcommunity.com/profiles/76561198036285290/recommended/2239150/)); "a last minute boss that ignores my walls and defenses" ([thronefall 190911975](https://steamcommunity.com/profiles/76561198079869714/recommended/2239150/)). AGREES with `fun-ai.md` #7 (composition test); NEW: King Mora's Royal Guard/Bombers/Second Wind checks must be beatable by at least three different draft archetypes, and shown on his rival card so no one learns "30 minutes later".
9. **Autonomous swarm units are the biggest control complaint in the genre: make critter intent visible and let the King fire while moving.** Necrosmith: 97 of 274 negatives (35%) [C] and The Unliving 59 of 182 (32%) are about unit AI and command ("NO PATROL OPTION, its really annoying watching your units just run away from the base they are meant to defend" ([necrosmith 120165504](https://steamcommunity.com/profiles/76561198019448195/recommended/1949190/))); Pikmin-likes: "You cant move while throwing your sprites, and it's really easy to get stunlocked" ([wildheart 121013697](https://steamcommunity.com/profiles/76561198043967997/recommended/1093290/)). Tooth and Tail's one-cursor model is loved by many but 27 negatives (9%) say it forces a death-ball ("your only command is 'Everybody follow me'" ([tnt 42592328](https://steamcommunity.com/profiles/76561198031354889/recommended/286000/))). AGREES with the one-control-layer pillar; NEW: garrison/hold/return needs a readable icon or colour state, and hold-to-fire must never slow or root the King.
10. **Unlocks as options: yes, but each of the first 10 must change a decision.** "It ties both raw power upgrades AND variety unlocks to the same currency" ([vcrawlers 225356970](https://steamcommunity.com/profiles/76561198015502624/recommended/3265700/)) (May 2026) supports GDD §7.5; Halls shows the opposite failure: "You can unlock all maps - pretty much nothing changes" ([halls 212784277](https://steamcommunity.com/profiles/76561197983617352/recommended/2218750/)). AGREES with `retention.md` #4; NEW: show unlock progress on the end screen (Brotato: "Learning late about the meta progression really soured my experience" ([brotato 188676496](https://steamcommunity.com/profiles/76561197988169749/recommended/1942280/))).
11. **Save the run after every battle.** 5 Halls reviews are rage about a crash near minute 25 of a 30-minute run; Bad North negatives call its multi-hour runs a problem ("way too long, multiple hours" ([badnorth 185483764](https://steamcommunity.com/profiles/76561198021187376/recommended/688420/))). Browser tabs close. NEW: persist run state (hero, Mutations, lives, next rival) to localStorage/IndexedDB after each draft, with a "Continue" button. Cheap, and no comparable game had it done well.
12. **The first hour is where most small games lose people; Kingswarm must sell itself by battle 1.** Share of negatives written at 2 hours or less: The Unliving 49%, Just King 47%, Tooth and Tail 38%, Bad North 32% vs 14-25% for the survivors-likes (Brotato 15%) [C from playtime]. AGREES with the "Instant" pillar and `onboarding-ux.md`; NEW: for the T&T-style failure ("you don't learn how to play, hints are useless" ([tnt 200108071](https://steamcommunity.com/profiles/76561198308798883/recommended/286000/))), battle 1 teaches three verbs and nothing else.

### Themes across all 7,194 coded reviews (share of each polarity) [C]
- **Negatives (n=2,794):** repetitive/boring 15%; difficulty/unfair/frustrating 13%; bugs/crashes/performance 11%; abandoned/no updates 6% (survivors-likes 9%, driven by 20MTD); meta-grind 6%; RNG/luck 6%; shallow/no-depth 6%; unit AI or bots 5% (commander games 12%); "too easy" 3%; run too long 3% by keyword but 49 by hand (0.7%), 30 of them Halls; controls 3%; not enough content 3%; dead multiplayer 2%; price 2% (Bad North 6%).
- **Positives (n=4,400):** build/synergy/replay 9%; "one more run"/addictive 8% (survivors-likes 17%, Vampire Survivors 25%, Vampire Crawlers 23%); value for money 7%; feels satisfying/smooth 7%; co-op/friends 6% (Rounds+Castle Crashers 16%); short-session praise 4%; cozy/"turn brain off" 6% (Pikmin-likes 23%).
- **By group (negatives):** survivors-likes lead with repetitive 22% and abandoned 9%; commander/strategy games with difficulty 19%, unit AI 12%, bugs 10%; duel/couch with bugs 14% (Rounds lag/latency, Castle Crashers patch bugs) and balance 12% (Rounds).

### One more run: what drives it [P]
- Build variety and improvisation: "every run keeps me coming back for just one more, trying more weird weapons and crazy combos" ([vs 212397080](https://steamcommunity.com/profiles/76561198206754344/recommended/1794680/)). Brotato build-praise 12% of positives [C].
- Getting better after death, and near-misses: "you just need to hold out for thirty more seconds" ([halls 225889465](https://steamcommunity.com/profiles/76561197973728816/recommended/2218750/)). Halls: "makes you want to improve with each death" ([halls 189383413](https://steamcommunity.com/profiles/76561198030393287/recommended/2218750/)). [I] this backs the near-miss end screen (GDD §7.4) and the 2-lives rematch.
- The pull dies when runs stop differing: "I felt like I was just repeating the same thing with slightly different numbers" ([brotato 234438435](https://steamcommunity.com/profiles/76561198100229372/recommended/1942280/)); Vampire Crawlers "doesn't have the 'one more run' pull" ([vcrawlers 231029974](https://steamcommunity.com/profiles/76561197965907850/recommended/3265700/)).
- The hardest self-report (1,055 upvotes): "i uninstalled the game. it was the only way i could stop playing" ([brotato 139295168](https://steamcommunity.com/profiles/76561198058240679/recommended/1942280/)).

### Repetition, depth, variety
- Repetitive is the #1 negative everywhere (15%). Survivors-likes: "Seems fun for the first 5-10 hours, but then becomes a meta knowledge check" ([brotato 162878029](https://steamcommunity.com/profiles/76561197995603452/recommended/1942280/)); "the game becomes a stand-still-for-30-minutes-and-win simulator" ([vs 190500868](https://steamcommunity.com/profiles/76561198269812804/recommended/1794680/)).
- Decisions: "this game has decisons. But such a small amount that they almost don't matter" ([vs 233152226](https://steamcommunity.com/profiles/76561198222397668/recommended/1794680/)) (Aug 2026), which HN then argued as "functionally an auto clicker" (https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49394836) while defenders said "you lose if you don't make the correct choices" (https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49393366) [secondary]. [I] hold-to-fire aim plus a King who can die is exactly the answer to "no agency".
- Too easy is a real quit reason for the newest entrant: Vampire Crawlers 27 of 200 negatives [C] ("I've never failed a run" ([vcrawlers 223844611](https://steamcommunity.com/profiles/76561198006013734/recommended/3265700/))). Threat to GDD §7.7 Assist: keep it opt-in and off by default.
- One-solution maps: Thronefall "every map has ONE solution that you have to find" ([thronefall 176228318](https://steamcommunity.com/profiles/76561198071430510/recommended/2239150/)); Tooth and Tail's random maps "lazy slop which means 'no mission is the same'" ([tnt 197693119](https://steamcommunity.com/profiles/76561197963508365/recommended/286000/)). [I] supports authored rivals with distinct counters (GDD §6) over procedural variety.

## GDD changes I would argue for
1. §7 / §8: add "Continue run" (save after each battle) and make Versus rounds host-selectable (3 or 5) with a rematch that keeps Mutations.
2. §4/§6: keep 22 Mutations as the floor, add bot-sim pick-rate gates, and budget a second layout or variant per rival for Crown Levels 4-8 (opponent variety is the complaint axis); keep Duo recipes hidden.
3. §7.5: every one of the first 10 unlocks must alter a decision (not a number or a skin); show unlock progress on the end screen; Crown Levels stay tactic-additive (Brotato Nightmare lesson).
4. §6: verify each rival and the Boss can be beaten by three or more draft archetypes; put counters on the rival card.
5. §3.2/§3.3: garrisoned/holding/returning critters get a visible state, and the King fires while moving at full speed (no root).
6. Distribution: launch ≤ $5 or free; no roadmap promises; complete 1.0.
7. §7.7 Assist stays opt-in, off by default (Crawlers "too easy" complaint).

## Where this disagrees with existing research
- `genre-teardown.md` #7 (15-25 min run) is at or above the tolerance players state (15-20); GDD's 12-15 is safer.
- `genre-teardown.md` #8 says T&T "bigger army always wins"; the community's larger T&T complaint is that the AI breaks the commander rules and the single-cursor cannot split attention (9% of negatives), not army size. [I]
- `retention.md` #9 (Hades-style assist) is right in principle but the newest survivors-like is criticised for being too easy (Crawlers), so keep it opt-in.
- `retention.md` treats Brotato's ladder as a model; players show the top rung can hurt (Nightmare, May 2026) if it changes the base game.
